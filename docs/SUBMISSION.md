## What the demo covers

1. Signing in with the seeded workspace and reading the analytics dashboard.
2. Server-side contact search, status filtering, keyset pagination, and streamed CSV import.
3. Building a campaign, selecting an audience, and dispatching through the provider abstraction.
4. Sending signed `opened` and `clicked` webhook events and watching analytics update.
5. Reviewing the public repository, README, and MySQL-backed CI pipeline.
